How to Delete Your Sakto Account and Data

Applies to Sakto accounts created with Apple, Google, or email-code sign-in.

In the app

Tap the circle with your initials at the top right of the main screen to open Your account, choose Delete account, and confirm. The app deletes your account, your player handle and the progress saved with it.

By email

If you can't use the app, email support@saktogames.app with the subject "Sakto account deletion request", sent from the email address associated with your account, or, if you signed in with Apple or Google, include your player handle so we can find the account. We'll verify the request is really from you, delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

What deletion covers

Deletion permanently removes from our servers: your account record and email address, your player handle, your gameplay progress (quiz results, Adventure progress, and free-sample records), and the purchase-entitlement records we hold. What's kept: purchase receipts are held by Apple / Google Play under their own policies (deleting your Sakto account doesn't refund purchases), and anonymized, aggregate analytics that can no longer identify you may be retained.

Guest players

If you played as a guest, your data lives only on your device: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. There's nothing on our servers to remove.

Questions

Anything unclear, or a request we haven't answered within 30 days: support@saktogames.app
