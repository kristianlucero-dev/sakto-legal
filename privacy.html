Sakto Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

Sakto is a trivia game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the game, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

Account information. Creating an account is optional. If you sign in (with Apple, Google, or a one-time email code), we store your email address and the player handle you choose. You can also play as a guest without an account, in which case your gameplay data stays on your device.

Gameplay data. Quiz results, scores, streaks, Adventure progress, and free-sample ("taste") plays, linked to your account so your progress syncs across devices. For guests, this data is stored only on the device.

Purchase status. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. We keep a record of which packs your account owns (via our purchase-management provider, RevenueCat, which assigns an app-specific identifier) so your content stays unlocked and can be restored.

Diagnostics and analytics. We use PostHog (product analytics, in anonymous mode) and Sentry (crash reporting) to understand aggregate usage and fix bugs (for example, which screens are used and where crashes occur). These tools receive technical data such as device model, OS version, app version, app-specific identifiers, and event logs. We do not use this data for advertising and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we don't do

No selling of personal data. No advertising SDKs, third-party advertising, or ad tracking of any kind. No collection of contacts, precise location, photos, or microphone data.

Where your data lives

Account and gameplay data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, on servers located in Singapore. Sign-in code emails are delivered by Resend. RevenueCat, PostHog, and Sentry process the data described above. All connections to these services are encrypted in transit, and each provider processes data only on our instructions to run the app.

Your choices and rights

You can play as a guest, and you can delete your account and associated data at any time. See our account and data deletion instructions; verified requests are completed within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access or correct your data; contact us and we'll honor them.

Children

Sakto is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and note the change in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@saktogames.app
